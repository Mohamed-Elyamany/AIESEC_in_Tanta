About Tanta & AIESEC

Discover the vibrant city of Tanta and how our local committee is driving youth leadership through life-changing global exchange opportunities.

Welcome to the Delta

Activating youth potential in the heart of Tanta through global experiences.

Tanta is more than just the capital of the Gharbia Governorate; it is a bustling hub of education, culture, and progress. Anchored by the dynamic student body of Tanta University, the city blends rich Egyptian history with the energetic drive of a generation ready to make an impact.

As an award-winning Local Committee, AIESEC in Tanta bridges the gap between local talent and global opportunities. Whether facilitating top-tier professional internships through our renowned Incoming Global Talent (IGTa/e) function or fostering deep social impact via Global Volunteer (IGV) projects, we provide the ultimate platform for cross-cultural understanding and leadership development.
